I am Anika Afrin, an adjunct lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Bangladesh. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University in 2023.

I teach Computer Architecture, Discrete Mathematics, Programming Language I, Data Communication, Digital Logic Design, and Numerical Methods. Previously, at Northern University Bangladesh, I also taught Algorithm Analysis and Design, Theory of Computation, Operating Systems, and Graphics Design. My academic responsibilities include assessment, thesis reviewing, and research supervision.

My research focuses on machine learning, generative AI, computer vision, and intelligent systems, with applications in healthcare, fraud detection, disaster management, and environmental assessment. I have experience in data preprocessing, feature extraction, model development, and evaluation using Python, NumPy, pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch, OpenCV, MATLAB, and Roboflow.

I welcome opportunities to collaborate with researchers on AI and interdisciplinary projects that address real-world challenges. I am particularly interested in combining complementary expertise to develop reliable and practical AI solutions. Please feel free to contact me to discuss shared research interests and potential collaborations.
