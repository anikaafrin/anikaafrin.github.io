I am Anika Afrin, an adjunct lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, Bangladesh. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University in 2023.

My work connects university teaching with applied artificial intelligence research. I have taught computing courses at BRAC University and Northern University Bangladesh and supported students through teaching assistance, thesis reviewing, and research supervision.

My research spans generative AI, computer vision, medical imaging, financial fraud detection, and intelligent systems. My publications include automated exam script checking with large language models, AeroGuard for fire and disaster management, and learning-based approaches to healthcare and environmental assessment.

I am interested in developing reliable and useful AI systems that address practical problems. My ongoing work includes privacy-preserving breast cancer diagnosis, multimodal disease risk prediction, deepfake detection, and conversational systems.

My technical background includes Python, Java, C, SQL, JavaScript, Django, MERN, MySQL, MATLAB, Roboflow, and Raspberry Pi.
